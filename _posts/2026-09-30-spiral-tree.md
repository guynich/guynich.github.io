Sunday walk with buddy Stefan in a county park on the Pacific Coastal Range.  My
map marked a "Spiral Tree" along the trail.  We took a look.

<img src="/images/spiral_tree.jpg" alt="Spiral Tree at Wunderlich in Woodside"
width="100%"/>

Under dense forest canopies, such as with coastal redwoods, this tree reaches
horizontally for light breaking through from above.  The trunk spirals in its
efforts.  Wonderful to witness.
